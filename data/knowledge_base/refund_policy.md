# Refund & Returns Policy

*Document type: policy | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-01-15*

## 1. Return Window

Products may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the conditions in Section 2 are met.

## 2. Condition Requirements

- Unopened, in original packaging: full refund, no restocking fee.
- Opened but unused, in resellable condition with all accessories: full refund minus a 10% restocking fee.
- Opened and used: not eligible for a standard return; may qualify for a warranty claim if defective (see Warranty Policy).

## 3. Return Shipping

- If the return is due to a Pearlz error (wrong item shipped, defective on arrival), Pearlz pays return shipping.
- If the return is due to customer preference (changed mind, ordered wrong model), the customer pays return shipping.

## 4. Refund Processing Time

| Payment Method | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Credit/debit card | 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item |
| Bank transfer | 7-10 business days |
| Cash on delivery (store credit only) | Immediate store credit upon inspection |

## 5. Non-Refundable Categories

- Filter cartridges once the packaging seal is broken
- Products marked "Final Sale" in the pricing guide
- Gift cards

## 6. Returns vs. Replacements vs. Warranty Repairs

- Return: you send the product back for a refund within the 14-day window.
- Replacement: you receive a new unit in exchange for a defective one, typically for issues found shortly after delivery (within 30 days), at no cost.
- Warranty repair: covered under the Warranty Policy for defects arising after the return window has closed but within the warranty period.

## 7. Promotional Bundles

If a bundle (e.g., unit + filter pack) is returned, the refund is calculated as the bundle price minus the value of any component not being returned, based on that component's individual list price.
