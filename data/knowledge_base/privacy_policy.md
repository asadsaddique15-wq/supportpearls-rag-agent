# Privacy Policy

*Document type: policy | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-01-05*

## 1. What Data We Collect

At purchase, we collect: name, delivery address, email, phone number, and payment details (processed by our payment partner, not stored by us directly).

Through the mobile app, we collect: device identifier, app usage logs, and water-usage telemetry (flow rate, filter status, alert history) from connected units.

## 2. Water-Usage Telemetry

Telemetry data is used to power the app's filter-life predictions and alert notifications. It includes flow volume, alert codes triggered, and firmware version. It does not include any audio, video, or location data.

## 3. Retention Periods

- Order and payment records: retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.
- App telemetry data: retained for 24 months, then automatically deleted.
- Marketing preferences: retained until you opt out.

## 4. Third Parties We Share Data With

- Courier partners: name, address, and phone number, for delivery purposes only.
- Payment processor: payment details, to process transactions.
- We do not sell personal data to advertisers or other third parties.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request deletion of your account and associated data.
- Request a copy (export) of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@pearlzhome.example.

## 6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for session management and basic analytics. You can disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings without affecting core site functionality.

## 7. Contact

For privacy-related requests or questions, contact privacy@pearlzhome.example.
