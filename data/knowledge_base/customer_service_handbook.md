# Customer Service Handbook

*Document type: guide | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-01-25*

## 1. Support Hours and Channels

Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, through email, the website contact form, and WhatsApp. There is no 24/7 support currently.

## 2. Escalation Matrix

| Priority | Definition | Response Target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 - Urgent | Safety issue (leak, sparking, burning smell) | Within 1 hour |
| P2 - High | Unit completely non-functional | Within 4 hours |
| P3 - Standard | General questions, filter/order queries | Within 24 hours |
| P4 - Low | Feedback, non-urgent suggestions | Within 3 business days |

## 3. Ticket Priority Definitions

Agents assign priority based on the customer's described symptom, not the customer's stated urgency. A P1 classification requires an actual safety-relevant symptom (leak, burning smell, sparking) as described in the Troubleshooting Guide's escalation section.

## 4. Tone of Voice Rules

- Be concise and plain — avoid internal jargon like "SKU" or "RMA" in customer-facing replies.
- Never guess at policy details; always reference the actual documented policy.
- Do not apologise excessively — one sincere acknowledgement is enough.

## 5. Complaint Handling Procedure

1. Acknowledge the complaint within the priority-appropriate window.
2. Reference the specific policy or documentation relevant to the complaint.
3. If the complaint cannot be resolved from documented policy, escalate to a senior agent rather than making an ad-hoc exception.

## 6. Compensation Authority Limits

Front-line agents may authorise:
- Refunds up to $50 without manager approval, if clearly justified by policy.
- One free replacement filter as a goodwill gesture, once per customer per year.

Anything beyond these limits requires senior agent or manager approval and is not something a front-line agent (or an automated assistant) should promise unilaterally.
