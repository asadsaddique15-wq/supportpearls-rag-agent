# Troubleshooting Guide

*Document type: guide | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-01-10*

## Audible Alert Codes (AquaPearl 500 Pro)

| Code | Beeps | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-01 | 1 long beep | Power supply fault | Check the power adapter connection; contact support if it persists |
| E-02 | 2 short beeps | Low water pressure | Check the inlet valve is fully open; verify household water pressure meets the minimum spec |
| E-03 | 3 short beeps | Sediment pre-filter saturated | Replace the sediment pre-filter cartridge; reset by holding RESET for 5 seconds |
| E-04 | 4 short beeps | Carbon filter saturated | Replace the carbon filter cartridge |
| E-05 | 5 short beeps | RO membrane needs replacement | Replace the RO membrane cartridge (see note on firmware 2.4 below) |
| E-06 | Continuous beeping | Leak detected at base | Stop use immediately and contact support; do not attempt self-repair |
| E-07 | 2 long beeps | App pairing timeout | Restart the unit and the app; ensure Bluetooth is enabled |
| E-08 | 1 short + 1 long beep | TDS sensor reading out of range | Contact support; may indicate a sensor or water-quality issue |

Note: firmware version 2.4 changed the meaning of alert code E-05. See the Product Changelog for details if your unit's firmware is unclear.

## Symptom-Based Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Probable Cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| No power / no lights | Adapter unplugged or faulty | Check power connection; try a different outlet |
| Low flow rate | Sediment filter saturated, or low water pressure | Check E-02/E-03 codes; replace filter if due |
| Leaking at the base | Loose fitting or damaged seal | Stop use; contact support (do not tighten fittings yourself while pressurised) |
| Bad taste in water | Carbon filter due for replacement | Replace carbon filter; if newly installed, see the Installation Guide's flushing procedure |
| Continuous running / won't shut off | Float valve stuck | Contact support; do not attempt to force the valve |
| Three beeps, LED flashing red | Sediment filter saturated (E-03) | Replace sediment filter and reset |
| TDS reading unusually high | RO membrane may be worn, or sensor fault | Check E-08 code; contact support if filter is not due |
| Noisy pump | Air trapped in the line after installation | Run the first-flush procedure again per the Installation Guide |
| App will not pair | Bluetooth or Wi-Fi issue | Restart unit and phone; ensure app is updated to the latest version |
| Water pressure feels weak even after filter change | Inlet valve partially closed | Fully open the inlet valve; check for kinks in supply tubing |

## When to Stop and Escalate

Do not attempt to open the unit, replace internal electronics, or repair a detected leak (E-06) yourself. Doing so voids the warranty per the Warranty Policy. Contact support for any of the following:
- Continuous leak alarm (E-06)
- Power supply fault (E-01) that persists after checking connections
- Any burning smell or visible sparking
- TDS sensor errors (E-08) after filter replacement
