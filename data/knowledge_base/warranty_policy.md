# Warranty Policy

*Document type: policy | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-02-01*

## 1. Warranty Period

- AquaPearl 300: 12 months from invoice date
- AquaPearl 500 Pro: 24 months from invoice date
- AeroPearl Mini: 18 months from invoice date
- AquaPearl 200 (discontinued): extended warranty of 6 months from the discontinuation date (31 Dec 2025), regardless of original purchase date

The warranty period is calculated from the invoice date, not the delivery date. If you cannot locate your invoice, contact support with your order confirmation email.

## 2. What Is Covered

The warranty covers the purifier or purifier body, pump, motor, and internal electronics against manufacturing defects under normal household use. If a covered part fails within the warranty period, Pearlz will repair or replace it at no charge.

## 3. Exclusions

### 3.1 Consumables

Filter cartridges, gaskets, and O-rings are classified as consumables and are excluded from the standard warranty. These wear out through normal use and are the customer's responsibility to replace.

### 3.2 Other Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage caused by drops, impact, or misuse
- Damage from unauthorised repair or disassembly by anyone other than a Pearlz-certified technician
- Commercial or industrial use of a residential-rated unit
- Damage caused by water quality outside the specified operating range (see the Product Manual for input water specifications)
- Damage caused by installation that does not follow the Installation Guide

## 4. Transferability

The warranty is transferable to a new owner if the unit is resold, provided the original invoice is presented. The warranty period continues to run from the original invoice date; it does not restart.

## 5. Claiming Warranty Service

To claim warranty service:
1. Contact support with your invoice number and a description of the fault.
2. Support will triage the issue and may ask you to attempt basic troubleshooting steps first.
3. If the fault is confirmed as a covered defect, a technician visit or replacement unit will be arranged.

The detailed claim procedure, including required documents and technician scheduling, is described in the Service & Maintenance Agreement.

## 6. Property Damage

The warranty covers the purifier itself. Claims for damage to surrounding property (e.g., cabinetry, flooring) caused by a unit malfunction are handled on a case-by-case basis and are not automatically covered — contact support to discuss.
