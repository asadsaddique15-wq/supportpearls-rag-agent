# Shipping Policy

*Document type: policy | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-01-20*

## 1. Delivery Timelines by Region

| Region Tier | Examples | Delivery Window |
|---|---|---|
| Metro | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad | 1-3 working days |
| Secondary city | Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar | 3-5 working days |
| Remote area | Skardu, Gilgit, Chitral | 5-7 working days |

## 2. Same-Day Dispatch

Orders placed before 2:00 PM on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after this cut-off are dispatched the next working day.

## 3. Free Shipping

Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders below this threshold are charged a flat $6.99 shipping fee.

## 4. Express and Next-Day Delivery

Next-day delivery is offered only in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad, for an additional $12.99. It is not available for remote-area addresses under any circumstances, including paid upgrades.

## 5. Fragile Item Packaging

Purifier units are shipped in reinforced double-walled boxes with corner protection. Replacement filters are shipped in padded mailers.

## 6. Failed Delivery Attempts

If delivery fails (customer unavailable, incorrect address), the courier will attempt delivery up to two more times over the following 3 working days. After three failed attempts, the order is returned to the warehouse and the customer is contacted to arrange re-delivery (additional shipping fee may apply) or a refund.

## 7. International Shipping

Pearlz does not currently offer international shipping. All orders are fulfilled within the country only.

## 8. Tracking

A tracking link is emailed once the order is dispatched. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to reflect the first scan.
