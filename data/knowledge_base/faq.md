# Frequently Asked Questions

*Document type: faq | Product line: all | Last updated: 2026-02-05*

## Ordering

**Q: How do I place an order?**
A: Order directly through our online store or through one of our retail partners.

**Q: Can I cancel an order after placing it?**
A: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are dispatched. Once dispatched, you'll need to use the standard return process.

**Q: Do you accept installment or EMI payments?**
Not covered in this FAQ.

## Delivery

**Q: How long will my order take to arrive?**
A: It depends on your region. See the Shipping Policy for exact timelines by area.

**Q: My machine keeps beeping three times and then stops, what does that mean?**
A: That's error code E-03, which means the sediment pre-filter is saturated and needs replacing. See the Troubleshooting Guide for the full fix.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
A: Yes, as long as the order hasn't been dispatched yet. Contact support with your order number.

## Installation

**Q: Do I need a plumber to install my AquaPearl 500 Pro?**
A: No, it's designed for self-installation. See the Installation Guide for step-by-step instructions. Professional installation is also available for a fee if you'd prefer.

**Q: Why does my water taste odd right after I installed the unit?**
A: This is normal and usually means the first-flush procedure wasn't completed. See the Installation Guide.

## Warranty

**Q: How long is the warranty?**

## App & Account

**Q: The app won't connect to my unit, what should I do?**
A: Restart both the unit and your phone, and make sure Bluetooth is enabled. See the Troubleshooting Guide for more steps.

**Q: How do I delete my account and data?**
A: Contact support to request account and data deletion. See the Privacy Policy for details on what data we hold and your rights.
